# Verification Logic

## Introduction

The Verification process ensures that the information and documents submitted by the beneficiary are genuine and complete before the application is approved.


## Verification Process

### Step 1

The eligible application is received by the verification team.


### Step 2

A Field Officer checks all uploaded documents.

Examples include:

- Aadhaar Card
- Income Certificate
- Address Proof
- Bank Details

### Step 3

The Field Officer confirms whether all documents are correct.

### If Documents are Correct

- The application is forwarded to the District Officer.
- The District Officer reviews the verification.
- If approved, the application moves to the Approval stage.

### If Documents are Incorrect

- Missing or incorrect documents are identified.
- The beneficiary is notified.
- The beneficiary uploads the corrected documents.
- The Field Officer verifies them again.

This process continues until all required documents are valid.

## Result

Only verified applications proceed to the Approval Workflow.
